## Findings

1. **t2.js, t2s2 question 10 ("why")** · STYLE (quotation over 14 words)
   - Material: "Technology can trigger interest because it is novel and exciting, but technology alone may not maintain it."
   - Source (R3, Digital Technology): "...can trigger interest because it is novel and exciting, but technology alone may not maintain interest" (15 consecutive identical words).
   - Fix: "Technology is new and exciting, so it can spark interest; on its own it may not keep that interest going."

2. **body2.html, passage "Looking ahead, the authors expect..."** · STYLE (quotation over 14 words)
   - Material: "...supportive conditions such as choice and optimal challenge can activate striatal areas associated with enjoyment and prefrontal processes critical to engaged learning".
   - Source (R2, section 7.1): identical run of 17 words ("supportive conditions such as choice ... enjoyment and prefrontal"), then "processes critical to engaged learning". Facts and the citation Reeve & Lee (2018) are correct.
   - Fix: "...brain research on intrinsic motivation already points to how choice and optimal challenge may switch on reward areas (the striatum) linked to enjoyment, and frontal areas needed for engaged learning (see Reeve & Lee, 2018)."

3. **body3.html, passage "Teachers can also encourage ties among the students themselves..."** · STYLE (near-verbatim, 11 words; also a repeat)
   - Material: "such an environment creates an interpersonal opportunity structure that supports belonging".
   - Source (R3, Cultivating Teacher-Student Connections): the same 11 words, with "interpersonal opportunity structure" in quotation marks. Under the 14-word limit but copied clause for clause.
   - Also: the sentence "Teachers can also encourage ties among students" now appears twice in the same paragraph (once in the older text, once in the new passage).
   - Also: "formal mentoring" is Larose et al. (2005) in the source; only Anderman (2003) is cited.
   - Fix: "The chapter calls a classroom like this an 'interpersonal opportunity structure' (Gray et al., 2018): one that gives students chances to connect." Delete the earlier duplicate half-sentence; cite "(Larose et al., 2005; Anderman, 2003)".

4. **body2.html, passage "Japan is one example..."** · STYLE (near-verbatim)

   - Fix: "...the more autonomously motivated pupils were more interested, studied more deeply and felt better about school. Promoting autonomy and intrinsic motivation also raised Japanese primary pupils' engagement in English lessons (Oga-Baldwin et al., 2017)."
