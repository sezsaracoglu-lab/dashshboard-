A difference worth knowing for the exam: reading 7 changed behavior with hardly any change in attitudes; reading 8 changed intentions by changing how a behavior connects to people's goals; readings 5 and 6 work through resources. Approaches that only appeal to the environment, or only reward and punish, have mixed effects at best (reading 8).

Use it on a case

The case clinic: diagnose first, then choose the move

🎯 Six workplaces, two decisions each. First decide what is going on (the diagnosis), then what the readings advise (the treatment). The cases are our invention; the diagnoses and moves come from the readings.

How to answer a case question. Ask three things in order. What is the symptom? (what people do or say). What is the diagnosis? (which mechanism from the readings is failing: knowledge, goals and feedback, the link with personal goals, resources, the leader's signal, a life-span shift, or stereotypes and climate). What is the treatment? (the practice from the readings that repairs it, with its condition or limit).

Real cases often have more than one cause. Name the one that fails first, and say how strong the evidence for your treatment is: a tested field study (reading 7) is stronger than a proposed model (readings 3, 4 and 6).
