## Remaining problems

1. **body.html step 8 tab ②, "So self-talk helped most..."**: the page says "the chapter suggests it should not be switched off abruptly but faded inward". The chapter (p. 61) says only that students "may need to be taught to fade overt verbalizations to a covert level". The word "abruptly" and the unhedged "should" are not in the chapter, so finding 11 is only partly resolved. **Fix:** "...and the chapter suggests students may need to be taught to fade it inward rather than simply stop."

2. **body.html step 7 tab ③, "Self-monitoring is one of the most researched processes (Mace et al., 1989; Zimmerman et al., 1996)"**: the chapter (p. 61) says "The effects of self-monitoring have been studied extensively". It gives no ranking. **Fix:** "The effects of self-monitoring have been studied extensively (...)".

3. **body.html step 10, curriculum sentence, "with students able to adapt the processes outside the instructional context"**: the chapter (p. 64) says "students can be taught to adapt self-regulatory processes for use on academic content outside of the instructional context". The page drops "can be taught to". **Fix:** "...and students can be taught to adapt the processes for use outside the instructional context".

4. **body.html verdict table, Operant row, "deferring an immediate reinforcer for a greater later one"**: the chapter (p. 46) says "different and usually greater". The table drops "usually", so the claim is stronger than the source. Body and scenes keep "usually". **Fix:** "...for a usually greater later one".

5. **body.html step 4, "built a model of self-regulated learning from information processing theory, which Greene and Azevedo (2007) consider very relevant for education"**: "which" can be read as pointing to the theory. The chapter (p. 49) calls the *model* highly relevant to education. **Fix:** "built an information processing model of self-regulated learning, which Greene and Azevedo (2007) consider very relevant for education".

6. **body.html step 5 tab ②, new sentence "In short, self-regulation depends heavily on..."**: together with the sentence before it, this forms a 15-word run copied from the chapter: "when they are unsuccessful, they may construct inappropriate goals and strategies. In short, self-regulation" (p. 54). **Fix:** replace "In short," with "So" (or "Put simply,").
