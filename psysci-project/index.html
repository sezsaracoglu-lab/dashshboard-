Group project guide

Our group experiment on memory for word lists, by a project group of four students. One page with what the course asks each week, the materials we were given, who does what, and the end product we hand in.

Next deadline: 1 December 2026. Upload the preregistration, consent form and ethics checklist as one file. We may not test any participant before that upload. Before then: check the word lists against the norms, pilot on ourselves, and fill in the yellow fields.

Our experiment

Immediate recallRecall after 3 min of sums
Randomized list8 participants do both
Categorized list8 participants do both

We measure how many of the 40 words a person writes down. Delay is compared within each person; list order is compared between the two groups.

  • 16participants, 4 per tester
  • 2 × 40words per session, two lists
  • 4 sper word, automatic
  • 3 minwritten recall per list
  • 20 minper session, in person
  • 2 × 2mixed ANOVA planned

Week 1

Design and team contract

DoneDeadline: 20 November 2026

What the course asks

  • Form the project team and agree how you work together.
  • Decide the 2 × 2 design for the experiment.
  • Sign the teamwork contract and upload it on Canvas.

Materials provided

  • Teamwork contract template (Canvas)
  • Meeting 1 worksheet: Experimental Design I
  • Passer, chapter 8: single-factor experimental designs

Our actions

  • AllKickoff meeting, contract signed and uploaded
  • AllConfirm the proposed design: every participant does both delay conditions, each participant gets one list order

Our end product

  • Signed teamwork contract, uploaded
  • Agreed design (proposal is in the preregistration draft)

Week 3

Preregistration, ethics and informed consent

Drafted, team check neededDeadline: 1 December 2026

What the course asks

  • Write the preregistration: Method plus Planned Analysis, maximum 900 words, one tense.
  • Create an informed consent form.
  • Fill out the ethics checklist together.
  • Combine the three into one file and upload it. No testing before this upload.

Materials provided

  • Instructions for writing a research report
  • APA style rules
  • Ethics checklist form
  • Consent form template, English and Dutch
  • Example preregistration and example report (Canvas)
  • Preparation for Meeting 3: van Zeijts et al. (2023), Passer chapter 3, Code of Ethics

Our actions

  • Member 2 and Member 1Finalize the preregistration after the pilot; go through the ethics checklist; upload the combined file
  • Member 3 and Member 4Finalize the consent form (contact email); lead recruitment
  • AllRecruit 4 participants each: 18 or older, not second-year Psychology students, unaware of the hypotheses

Our end product

  • One Word file: preregistration (860 words), consent form, ethics checklist
  • 16 participants lined up

Open decisions

  • Confirm the design proposal as a group. If it changes, the decks and the preregistration change too.
  • The 80 words are a draft. Each must be checked as a low-frequency answer in the Van Overschelde et al. (2004) norms before the upload.
  • Do we add our predictions to the planned analysis, as the course example does?
  • Keep or delete the cover page of the upload file (the course example has none).

Questions for the tutor

  • The Meeting 6 instructions say "analyze the combined group data". What is combined, and does it fix any part of our procedure?
  • Is recruiting participants of 18 and older acceptable? Our contract says 16 and older; the ethics checklist treats under 18 as a vulnerable group.
  • What is the date of the final report deadline?

How this guide stays current

Each week the group brings the new course materials and our progress to Claude, which prepares the next drafts and updates this page and the checklist. Claude drafts and checks; the team decides, tests participants and submits.
