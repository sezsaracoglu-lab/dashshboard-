Leadership

Agency begins where observation ends

Sezen Saracoglu17 February 20261 min read

I have seen leadership teams where everyone was capable, informed, and committed.
The strategy was sound.
The intent was shared.

Yet certain decisions remained suspended.
Not because they were unclear, but because claiming them would attach consequence.

Agency is the moment someone accepts that proximity.

Over time, I began to see the difference it creates.
When leaders operate with agency, progress accelerates.
Not because everything is agreed, but because someone is willing to carry the next step.

When agency is absent, conversations continue. Ownership does not.

It matters because leadership is not defined by what you oversee.

It is defined by what you are willing to move forward without waiting for permission to feel safe.

Agency is what turns leadership from a position into a force.

Sezen Saracoglu, PCC
