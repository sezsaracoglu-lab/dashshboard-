The Founding Myth: do we still remember why we are here?

Sezen Saracoglu25 August 20261 min read

A team without memory operates on habit.
A team that remembers its origin operates on purpose.

Every team has a founding story. A reason people originally came together. A spark of shared purpose, admiration, or belief in something that mattered.

Over time, that story gets buried. Under deadlines, restructures, and the weight of daily operations. The team forgets. Not the facts, but the feeling.

In relationship systems work, this is called the Founding Myth. Not myth as in fiction. Myth as in the deep, meaningful narrative that holds a system together.

In over twenty years of working with senior leaders, I have seen this pattern repeatedly. The teams that lose momentum rarely lose it because of strategy. They lose it because they have forgotten why they came together.

The questions that bring it back are disarmingly simple:

What originally drew you to this work?
What did you admire about the people around this table?
What was the belief that made you say yes?

This is not nostalgia. This is system repair.

Before you redesign your team's way of working, ask a simpler question first:
Do we still remember why we are here?

If the answer is unclear, start there.
Everything else follows.
