# World of Work · Theme 1 · high-yield blueprint for the meeting sets

This is an evidence-based list of what is most worth practising, not a prediction of the exam. No past exams for this course were available, so the ranking rests on the learning goals of meeting 1 [LO], what is central in each reading [CORE], and key terms an exam can define [LEX]. Ranking: LO + CORE > CORE + LEX > CORE.

## Meeting 1 · readings 1 and 2 (sets m1a, m1b)

1. Job demands and job resources: definitions, and "a low resource is not a demand, a low demand is not a resource" [LO] [CORE]
2. The two processes: health impairment and motivational [LO] [CORE]
3. Buffer and boost: which outcome each works on, and how each looks in a graph [LO] [CORE]
4. Figure 1 of the FAQ: main effect versus interaction, restricted range of demands [CORE]
5. Personal resources: beliefs, not behaviours; reciprocal with job resources; moderators of demands [LO] [CORE]
6. Challenge versus hindrance demands [LO] [CORE]
7. Burnout: exhaustion, cynicism versus depersonalization, ICD-11, overlap with depression [LEX] [CORE]
8. Work engagement: vigor, dedication, absorption; two views; not satisfaction, not workaholism [LO] [LEX]
9. Engagement is not the opposite of burnout: two measures, "low on both" [LO] [CORE]
10. Causes of burnout and engagement in the meta-analyses (Aronsson, Alarcon, Lesener, Mazzetti) [LO] [CORE]
11. Consequences: Swider and Zimmerman's three links, other-rated performance, health and mortality [CORE]
12. Loss and gain cycles and spirals: two criteria, the evidence, why it is thin [LO] [CORE]
13. Person × situation and multilevel JD-R: what is nested in what, trickle-down, team studies [LO] [CORE]
14. COR in the FAQ: gain paradox, the COR paradox, breadth of "resources" [LO]
15. Nine testable predictions: mediation versus moderation; falsifiability [CORE] [LEX]
16. Evidence types: meta-analysis, diary (within-person), longitudinal; within versus between [CORE]
17. Measurement in two stages; culture (4 of 24 tests) [CORE]
18. Three proactive behaviours: job crafting, proactive vitality management, playful work design [CORE]
19. Work–home resources model: volatile personal resources; cross-domain buffering [CORE]
20. Table 2 (common mistakes) and the routes for practice; most interventions are individual [CORE]
21. The 2014 agenda checked: 191 diary studies, job crafting as missing link, few observer studies [CORE]
